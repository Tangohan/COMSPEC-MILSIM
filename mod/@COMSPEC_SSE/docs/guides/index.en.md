# COMSPEC SSE tool guides

One complete guide per tool: what it is for, where to find it (Zeus, Eden, ACE, ATAK phone, Esc menu), a step-by-step on a gameplay example, every field and setting, common pitfalls, link with Athena. The same content, in shorter form, is in the game: **MANUEL SSE** (SSE MANUAL) (Esc › Field Manual › COMSPEC SSE; in Zeus: the « Ouvrir le manuel » (Open the manual) module; in Eden: the COMSPEC SSE menu; in every SSE window: the « ? » button). French index: [index.md](index.md).
