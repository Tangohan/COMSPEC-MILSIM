# SSE Zeus and Eden modules

French version: [modules_zeus_eden.md](modules_zeus_eden.md). Guides index: [index.en.md](index.en.md). Tutorials and generation modules in detail: [../guide/Modules_and_equipment_SSE.en.md](../guide/Modules_and_equipment_SSE.en.md) (some names there are old: the exact names are below).

## What it is for

Three module categories, shared by Eden (Systems › Modules) and Zeus (ZEN): **COMSPEC · Renseignement (SSE)** (COMSPEC · Intelligence (SSE)) (generation, sites, organisations, editor), **COMSPEC · Identité** (COMSPEC · Identity) (papers, checkpoints, covers, HVT, BOLO) and **COMSPEC · Mission** (campaign, case, sheet presentation, SEEK, seals). To these are added **COMSPEC · IA RP** (COMSPEC · RP AI) (free text with the AI) and, in Eden, the attributes of units, groups and objects.

## Step by step: an identity mission in Eden

1. **Charger une campagne** module (COMSPEC · Mission): identifier `OP-SABLE`, type Permanente.
2. On each key civilian: attributes **COMSPEC SSE — Papiers** (Papiers portés, Nature, Profil au contrôle) and **— Biométrie** (Fichier biométrique, Identifiant biométrique).
3. **Poser un checkpoint IA** module at the bridge, guards synced, Équipement Scanner MRZ, Sévérité Normale.
4. The infiltrated player: attribute **Infiltré (couverture)**, Nature **Couverture préparée**.
5. Menu bar › COMSPEC SSE › **Donner un document** for an abandoned paper (target Poser au sol).
6. **Fixer la référence du dossier** module: `SSE-2026-0007`. Tableau de bord SSE to check.

## Common pitfalls

- The Zeus identity modules go through the server and check that the requester is Zeus; a Zeus **on a client machine** does not see the nature of forged papers nor the true identity in « Régler la biométrie » (fields left on « Inchangé » (Unchanged)).
- **Poser un checkpoint IA** is an Eden module only for the editor; in Zeus it is the ZEN entry of the same name.
- The **active case** no longer has a ZEN entry: **Fixer la référence du dossier** module or SSE editor (Dossier (Case) type).
- The old names (« Générer un profil SSE » (Generate an SSE profile), « Campagne SSE : sauvegarder » (SSE campaign: save), « Tutoriel : manuel du Zeus » (Tutorial: Zeus manual)) refer to the current modules (« Générer un profil », « Sauvegarder la campagne », « Ouvrir le manuel »).
- **Doter d'un terminal SEEK**: 25 m by default in Eden, 30 m in Zeus.
- ZEN modules need **Zeus Enhanced**; without ZEN, only the config modules (including Éditeur SSE (SSE editor)) are available in Zeus.

## Athena

Connexion Athena (Eden menu bar) links the machine; Campagnes (send / fetch), Référence du dossier (Case reference), AI profiles and kits (Caisse de kits (Athena)) go through COMSPEC Link. The Eden Tableau de bord SSE is read-only and sends nothing.
